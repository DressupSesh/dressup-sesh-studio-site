How to take Poshmark photos with your phone

A simple photo checklist for the next piece you want to list.

Dressup Sesh Studio · Updated November 9, 2026

What photos should you take?

Photograph the full front and back, then add close-ups of the label, material, construction and any flaws. Use bright, even light and leave space around the item. Check that the color and condition look true to life before you prepare your cover.

What shape should the photo be?

Poshmark’s March 2026 guide describes portrait 3:4 listing photos. Keep the full garment centered and review how the cover looks in its frame.

What should cleanup change?

Remove distracting surroundings while preserving the item’s real details. Check edges, straps, fringe and any areas where the item and background have similar colors.

Source: Poshmark’s official portrait-photo guide.
