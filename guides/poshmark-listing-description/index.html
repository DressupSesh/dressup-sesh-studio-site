What should a Poshmark listing description include?

A listing checklist that starts with the item in your hands.

Dressup Sesh Studio · Updated November 9, 2026

What belongs in the title?

Use the brand, item type, style name when known, size and relevant color. Poshmark’s listing help page gives an 80-character title limit. Use accurate identifying details before decorative language, and verify every generated detail against your item.

What belongs in the description?

Include condition, measurements you actually took, tag size, fabric content shown on the label, construction details and any flaws. Do not invent a material or original retail price from appearance alone.
