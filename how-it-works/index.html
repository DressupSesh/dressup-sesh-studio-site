Review. Save. Post.

  1. Give it your final look.

    Edit the photos and words, then check the review box.

    Save photos to your phone or download them individually on a computer. Your title and description copy together.

  3. Choose your marketplace.

    Open Poshmark, eBay, Depop, Etsy or Vinted. Add your saved photos, paste the copy and finish posting there.
