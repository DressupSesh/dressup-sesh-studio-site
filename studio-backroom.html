    STRIPE RECONCILIATION

    Fees & refund decisions

    Open Stripe payments ↗

    Issue refunds in Stripe. Sync brings existing payments, refund statuses and actual fees into the Back Room. Successful membership refunds automatically suspend new workflow use after import. Cancel subscriptions in Stripe separately to stop renewal. Creative-pack credits use the bounded review below.

    Refunds & credits

    1. Open Fees & sync and click Sync Stripe records to import the payment and its refund.
    2. Return here and find the succeeded refund for the creative credit pack.
    3. Click Remove N unused pack credits, confirm the amount, then check the saved review below.

    Review refunded creative packs before removing unused purchased credits. Used credits and unrelated trial or monthly credits remain intact. A successful refund of the current membership invoice suspends all three workflows and prompts the user to subscribe again. Stored trial bonuses and unrelated purchased credits are preserved but unavailable while suspended.

    No removal button? The refund may still be pending, be a subscription refund, lack a linked purchase, or have no eligible unused pack credits. A Studio account labeled Test still qualifies when its Stripe payment is live. Membership access is suspended automatically for a successful current membership refund. Cancel the subscription in Stripe separately to stop renewal. This removal control handles purchased creative pack credits only.
