STRIPE RECONCILIATION

Fees & refund decisions

Open Stripe payments ↗

Issue refunds in Stripe. Sync brings existing payments, refund statuses and actual fees into the Back Room. Refunds do not automatically cancel subscriptions or remove credits.

Refunds & credits

  1. Open Fees & sync and click Sync Stripe records to import the payment and its refund.
  2. Return here and find the succeeded refund for the creative credit pack.
  3. Click Remove N unused pack credits, confirm the amount, then check the saved review below.

Review refunded creative packs before removing unused purchased credits. Used credits and unrelated trial or monthly credits remain intact. Subscription refunds require a separate access decision in Stripe.

No removal button? The refund may still be pending, be a subscription refund, lack a linked purchase, or have no eligible unused pack credits. A Studio account labeled Test still qualifies when its Stripe payment is live. Subscription refunds must be handled by cancelling the subscription in Stripe; this control removes purchased creative pack credits only.
