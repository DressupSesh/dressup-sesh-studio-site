YOUR STUDIO, STEP BY STEP

Make it. Check it. Save it.

Updated November 13, 2026

Work on one item at a time. Review the results, save your photos, and copy your listing before starting the next item.

  1. Choose your tool
  2. Start with clear photos
  3. Review and refine
  4. Save on phone or computer
  5. Clear, resume, or start again
  6. Post and manage your account

1. Choose your tool

Photo Cleanup
Upload your product photos, then select Remove Backgrounds. Use the editing controls to refine the result.
Listing Copy
Reuses the photos from Photo Cleanup. Add facts in Optional item details, generate the copy, and check it before copying.
Creative Images
Reuses your product photos. Choose Ghost Mannequin, On the Body, Influencer Post, or Editorial. An optional inspiration photo guides the pose, setting, or layout.
Listing Upgrade · owner pilot
Currently available in the owner test account. Paste a supported item link or add photos and details manually. Compare the original listing with one new ghost mannequin main photo and refreshed title and description.
Batch Pro · owner pilot
Work across multiple items, review each result, and save the intended item or selection. Public availability is coming soon.

2. Start with clear photos

  • Use a full view of the item with even light and enough space around its edges. Add the back, close-ups, and any flaws.
  • Include readable brand, size, fabric, and measurement photos. Label them where the tool offers labels.
  • Use Reference only for information photos that should inform copy without being cleaned or used as the product in Creative Images.
  • Keep photos of different items separate. Clear the current item before uploading the next one.

For Listing Upgrade, imported photos depend on what the marketplace makes available. If the main photo is cropped or the import is incomplete, use the manual option with a clear full-item photo and supporting views. The output is one new main photo.

3. Review and refine

Compare the result with your original item. Check color, pattern, shape, neckline, sleeves, logos, fastenings, and visible wear. A creative image should still represent what the buyer receives.

Verify the brand, size, measurements, material, condition, and any retail-price or age claims in the copy. Correct unsupported details before posting. Use clear directions when regenerating; a new generation may use allowance.

In Listing Upgrade, edit the title and description directly. Put any details you want pasted into those fields. Check the review box to enable Quick Save; editing afterward requires another review.

4. Save on phone or computer

On your phone

  1. In Photo Cleanup or Creative Images, tap Save All to Phone or the phone icon.
  2. On iPhone, choose Save Image or Save Images in the share sheet. Check the Photos app afterward. Choosing Save to Files saves to Files instead.
  3. On Android, choose the available save or download option. Check Gallery, Photos, or Files; the destination depends on the option and browser.
  4. Copy your listing text using the copy button. Saving photos in the three standard tools does not also copy the text.

If sharing several photos is unavailable, use the available individual-photo save controls or download the ZIP. Extract a ZIP in Files before using its photos.

On your computer

  1. Use the download controls for photos or creatives. The computer icon at the top downloads the available item assets together as a ZIP, including listing text when present.
  2. Find the file in your browser’s downloads or the folder you selected. Open a ZIP to extract its contents.
  3. Use the copy button when you want to paste the listing immediately.

A download saves a file to your computer. It does not automatically add it to the Photos app.

Listing Upgrade: one Quick Save

Quick Save exports the new main photo and copies the title + description together. On iPhone, finish the photo save in the share sheet. On a computer, the photo download starts.

The checkmark acknowledges the export request and copied text. “Photo exported” means the share action completed; “Photo download started” means the browser began the download. Check the destination you selected to confirm the file is there.

If you cancel or copying is blocked, use the retry button or Help with saving or copying. You can save the photo or copy the text separately there. The clipboard holds your most recently copied content; copying something else replaces it.

5. Clear, resume, or start again

Clear All · New Item
In the three standard tools, this clears the current workspace’s uploaded photos, generated images, listing text, reference, and instructions. Save and copy first, then confirm. Files already saved on your device remain there.
Start new listing
In Listing Upgrade, a saved draft stays in Recent drafts. Reopen it to continue editing. A saved draft is separate from a photo exported to your device.
Resume
If an Upgrade result is missing or processing was interrupted, reopen the draft and select Resume. Let Studio check existing jobs before starting another generation.
Switching apps or refreshing
Save finished work before refreshing, signing out, or clearing browser data. If you return to a pending job, give Studio time to check its status. Do not repeatedly submit the same request.

If a photo or result still will not load, keep the draft and contact support with the tool, approximate time, and a screenshot. Avoid clearing the item as a first troubleshooting step.

6. Post and manage your account

After saving, open your marketplace, add the exported photo, and paste the copied text. Some marketplaces require the title and description in separate fields; Listing Upgrade’s Help area contains the combined text for manual copying.

In the owner pilot, the Poshmark button in Quick Save and Batch opens the app on phones. The Poshmark website fallback is under Help in Quick Save. Desktop uses the website. Other marketplace buttons currently link to their websites. Review and publish inside the marketplace; Studio does not publish for you.

Use the same account each time to keep your plan and work together. Phone-number sign-in is not yet publicly available. Open Plan → Manage billing for membership and cancellation options.

Need help? Visit Support or email support@dressupsesh.studio. Include your account email, tool, device, browser, and approximate time. Never send passwords or verification codes.
